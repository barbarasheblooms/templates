Hiring Plan vs. Runway Calculator
Check that your hiring plan still fits inside your runway before you commit to it
Before you start: a hiring plan built in a spreadsheet in isolation can quietly wreck your runway. This tool adds your planned hires' fully-loaded cost to your current burn and recalculates how many months of cash you actually have left, so you commit to a plan you can afford rather than one you hope you can afford.
