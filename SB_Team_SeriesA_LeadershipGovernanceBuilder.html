Board Composition Calculator
Check your board balance the way governance-minded investors do
Before you start: by Series A your board stops being a formality and starts being a real check on how the company is run. Investors read your board composition as a signal of how decisions get made when things get hard. This tool calculates your founder board percentage and checks whether your board is investor-majority, the two things governance-minded investors look at first.

Your board seats
What it is: seats held by founders or co-founders of the company. How to calculate: count seats currently held by founders under your shareholders' agreement or board resolution, not advisory or observer seats.
What it is: seats held by investors, usually one per lead investor per round. How to calculate: count seats currently allocated to VCs or other institutional investors under your cap table's board rights.
What it is: seats held by neither a founder nor an investor, typically an outside operator or domain expert. How to calculate: count seats filled by someone with no equity stake tied to a specific investor and no operating role at the company.
