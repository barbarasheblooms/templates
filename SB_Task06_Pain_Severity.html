Pain Severity Calculator
Pain severity is not a feeling — it's a measurement. Score each interviewee across three dimensions: frequency, impact, and urgency. A score of 10+/15 with impact ≥4 counts as high severity. You need 70%+ of your ICP interviews to reach this threshold to pass the Stage 1 gate.
70%
Stage 1 gate threshold
At least 70% of your ICP interviews must score 10+/15 with impact ≥4 to pass. Below 70% means the problem may not be urgent enough — or your ICP is too broad.
How to score each dimension
Ask these questions during the interview. Score 1–5 for each.
Frequency
How often does this problem happen?
1Rarely / can't remember when
2A few times a year
3Monthly
4Weekly
5Daily / every time they do X
Impact ⚠
How much does it cost? (time, money, opportunity)
1Minor annoyance, no cost
2Small cost, easy to ignore
3Noticeable cost, some workaround
4Serious cost — named hours or €
5Major blocker — lost clients / revenue
Urgency
How motivated are they to fix it now?
1"I'd try it someday"
2Passive interest
3Would try a free solution
4Actively looking for a solution
5"When can I use it?" / offered to pay
