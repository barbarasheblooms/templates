Financial Planning Worksheet
Turn your real revenue, cost and growth assumptions into a 12-month plan and the health metrics that come with it
Before you start: a financial plan built from a target ("we want to raise €X") reads differently than one built from your real numbers forward. Enter your actual current MRR, your actual monthly costs by bucket, and the growth rate you can defend, and this tool builds the 12-month plan and the metrics an investor would check on top of it.
