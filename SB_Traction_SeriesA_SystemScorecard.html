Growth Predictability Score
Check whether your growth is a repeatable system or a lumpy series of one-off wins
Before you start: by Series A, investors aren't just asking "did you grow", they're asking "will this keep happening without you personally closing every deal". A growth line that jumps around month to month, even if the average is good, reads as luck. This tool calculates the coefficient of variation across your last 4 months, the standard statistical way to measure how predictable a series of numbers actually is.
