Turn your price and first customers into the three numbers investors actually check
Before you start: at Pre-Seed you rarely have clean, mature unit economics, and that's fine. What matters is that you calculate them honestly from your real (even if small) numbers, rather than skipping straight to a use-of-funds slide with no math behind it. This tool computes your LTV:CAC ratio, CAC payback period, and runway from what you enter, and tells you where each one sits against the benchmark investors use.
