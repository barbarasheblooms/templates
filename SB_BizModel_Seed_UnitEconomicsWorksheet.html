Unit Economics & Margin Worksheet
Turn your gross margin into the LTV your Seed round actually needs
Before you start: by Seed, investors expect your unit economics to be margin-adjusted, not just top-line. A customer paying you €50/month isn't worth €50/month to your LTV if it costs you €20/month to actually serve them. This tool takes your gross margin, folds it into your LTV:CAC ratio and CAC payback, and tells you exactly where each one sits against the benchmarks investors screen Seed decks against.

Price and cost to serve
€/mo
What it is: what one customer pays you each month. How to calculate: use your current listed or actual price; if billing is annual, per-seat, or usage-based, convert it to a single monthly-equivalent number (annual price ÷ 12, or seats × price per seat).
€/mo
What it is: the direct monthly cost of delivering your product to one customer: hosting, support, payment processing. How to calculate: total monthly COGS (hosting + support + payment processing, not salaries or marketing) ÷ active customers this month.
2
Acquisition and retention
€
What it is: what it costs, on average, to turn a stranger into a paying customer. How to calculate: total sales & marketing spend over a period ÷ new paying customers acquired in that same period. Include ad spend, tools, and a fair share of your own time if you're doing sales yourself.
%
What it is: the share of customers who cancel in a typical month. How to calculate: customers lost this month ÷ customers at the start of the month × 100. Use a 3-month average if one month was unusual.
