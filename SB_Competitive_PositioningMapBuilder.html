Competitive Positioning Map Builder

Plot yourself and up to 6 competitors on a 2x2 map. Choose axes that reflect what your customer actually weighs when choosing — not "innovative vs. not innovative."

Before you start: pick axes that are decision-relevant to your customer (price, implementation time, depth of automation, self-serve vs. high-touch). Vague axes produce a vague map.

Your positioning map
Each point is placed using its horizontal and vertical slider values. Look for the quadrant where you sit alone, or where you and only weak/indirect competitors sit — that's the position worth explaining explicitly on your competitive slide.
