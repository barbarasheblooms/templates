Scale Economics: Magic Number & Rule of 40
The two efficiency metrics Series A investors actually run on your numbers
Before you start: at Series A, investors stop asking "are you growing" and start asking "how efficiently are you buying that growth." The Magic Number tells them how much ARR your sales & marketing spend is producing right now. The Rule of 40 tells them whether your growth rate and your profitability, taken together, clear a bar that's become a standard shorthand for a healthy SaaS business. This tool calculates both from your real quarterly numbers.

Growth and profitability
%
What it is: how much your revenue grew over the last full year. How to calculate: (revenue this year − revenue last year) ÷ revenue last year × 100.
%
What it is: how profitable the business is, as a share of revenue. How to calculate: (revenue − total costs) ÷ revenue × 100. This can be negative if you're still burning to grow, that's fine, enter the negative number.
