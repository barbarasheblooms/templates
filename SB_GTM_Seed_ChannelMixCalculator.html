Channel Mix & Concentration Calculator
Check whether your growth depends on one channel more than it should
Before you start: at Seed, investors don't just want to know that you have customers, they want to know if losing your single biggest channel tomorrow would sink the business. This tool checks how concentrated your customer base is in one channel, and calculates exactly how many customers from other channels you'd need to bring that concentration back to a healthy level.
