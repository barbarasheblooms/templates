Score your founding team's coverage across the skills investors check for
Before you start: investors don't expect one founder to be excellent at everything, they expect the founding team, together, to cover the core functions a company needs in its first year. This tool scores your team honestly against four of those functions and tells you exactly where the real gaps are.
