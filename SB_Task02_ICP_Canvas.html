ICP Definition Canvas
The ICP is not a marketing concept — it's a validation tool. It tells you who to interview, what to build first, and how to talk about it. A good ICP is specific enough that you could find 50 matching people on LinkedIn in under an hour. "Female founders" is a target market. "First-time female founders, pre-revenue, B2B, preparing to raise in the next 12 months" is an ICP.

When it happens

Current solution
What do they do today? Who are they paying? What workarounds have they built?
05
Why the current solution fails them
This is your competitive differentiation. What gap do existing solutions leave? What do they still wish they had?
06
Buying trigger
What specific event makes them look for a solution right now?
07
What success looks like
What does their life look like after the problem is solved? What do they actually want?
08
Where to find them
Online communities, events, newsletters, LinkedIn groups. Be specific enough to build a list.

The ICP definition test
Answer these four questions before committing to this ICP.
1. Can I find them on LinkedIn in under an hour?
2. Do they demonstrably have the problem from interviews?
3. Do they have the ability and motivation to pay?
4. Are there 1,000+ matching people in your region?
✦ Evidence required: ICP definition with at least 5 completed interviews. Inorganic demand signals documented. All four ICP tests answered.
