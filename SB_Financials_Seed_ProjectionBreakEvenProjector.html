Break-Even Projector
Project the month you stop burning cash, from your real growth rate
Before you start: "we'll be profitable eventually" is not a number investors can check. A break-even month, projected honestly from your current MRR, burn, and growth rate, is. This tool runs the actual compounding math on your numbers instead of asking you to guess.

What it is: your active recurring revenue this month. How to calculate: sum of active recurring revenue this month; exclude one-time revenue like setup fees or one-off consulting.

What it is: how much cash leaves your account each month, net of revenue. How to calculate: total monthly expenses minus monthly revenue collected. Use your last 1-3 months' average, not one unusual month.
%
What it is: how fast your MRR is growing, month over month. How to calculate: (this month's MRR − last month's MRR) ÷ last month's MRR × 100, averaged over the last 3 months so one unusual month doesn't skew the projection.
