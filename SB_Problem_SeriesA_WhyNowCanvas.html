Market Timing Growth Calculator
Prove the window is still open, with your category's actual growth rate
Before you start: "why now" is one of the weakest slides in most Series A decks because it's usually a narrative with no number attached. Investors have seen enough categories peak and stall to want proof the window is genuinely still open, not just a founder's confidence that it is. This tool takes a category-wide metric from a credible source, two years apart, and turns it into an annualized growth rate you can defend, then checks it against your own growth.

How to calculate: pull the figure from an industry report, government statistic, or platform-published data point from exactly 2 years before today, and note the source and date so you can cite it when asked.
