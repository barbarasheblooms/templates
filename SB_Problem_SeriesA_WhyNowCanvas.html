Why Now: Timing Argument Canvas
Structure the evidence that the window for this problem is still open
Before you start: pick the force that best explains why this is the moment to fund this company, then back it with a date, a source, and a trend. A timing argument without a number reads as an opinion. This canvas is built to force the number.
1
Which force opens your window?
Choose the one that applies most directly. If more than one applies, fill in the strongest and note the others below.
Regulatory / structural shift
A law, standard, or requirement changed, creating a new obligation or removing a barrier.
Behaviour change
How people work, buy, or decide has shifted, making your solution newly relevant.
New technology / infrastructure
A new capability makes a previously uneconomical solution viable for the first time.
