ICP Definition Canvas
Define who has the problem specifically enough to find, interview and size a market from
Before you start: your ICP is not a marketing concept, it's what the rest of the Market slide is built on. "Female founders" is a target market. "First-time female founders, pre-revenue, B2B, raising in the next 12 months" is an ICP specific enough that you could find 50 matching people on LinkedIn in under an hour.

The ICP definition test
Answer these four questions before committing to this ICP and moving on to sizing your market.
1. Can I find them on LinkedIn in under an hour?
2. Do they demonstrably have the problem from interviews?
3. Do they have the ability and motivation to pay?
4. Are there enough matching people in your region to build a SOM from?
✦
What this needs to include: an ICP specific enough to find, interview and count, backed by at least a few completed interviews, with demand signals and open assumptions documented.
