Runway & Use of Funds Calculator
Turn your raise into a runway number and a fund allocation investors can check
Before you start: investors read a use-of-funds slide two ways: does the runway number match the raise and burn you stated elsewhere in the deck, and do the percentages you've allocated actually add up to 100%. Both are simple arithmetic checks, and both get skipped more often than you'd think. This tool runs both checks on your real numbers.

What it is: how much cash leaves your account each month, net of revenue. How to calculate: total monthly expenses minus monthly revenue collected. Use your last 1-3 months' average, not one unusual month.

Use of funds (must total 100%)
%
What it is: the share of the raise going to product and engineering: salaries, tools, infrastructure. How to calculate: estimated monthly cost of that team × months funded by this raise ÷ raise amount × 100.
%
What it is: the share of the raise going to sales and marketing. How to calculate: estimated monthly S&M cost × months funded ÷ raise amount × 100.
%
What it is: the share going to operations, admin, legal, and other overhead. How to calculate: estimated monthly ops/G&A cost × months funded ÷ raise amount × 100.
%
What it is: the unallocated cushion you keep for surprises. How to calculate: same method, estimated monthly buffer amount × months funded ÷ raise amount × 100. Most rounds keep 10-20% here.
