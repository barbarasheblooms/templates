Well-Funded Entrant Stress Test
A structured way to pressure-test your defensibility if a funded competitor copies you tomorrow, not an industry-standard benchmark
Before you start: this composite index is a structured way to think through the question "what happens if a well-funded competitor copies us tomorrow", not a recognized external metric like LTV:CAC or a moat score built on named categories. Use it to force an honest answer on three concrete inputs, then treat the resulting number as a talking point, not a certified score.
1
Three honest inputs
What it is: your honest estimate of how long it would take a well-funded, motivated competitor to build a working copy of your core product. How to calculate: think through the engineering, data, and integration work involved, and give your real estimate in months, capped conceptually around 24 months (beyond that, treat it as "hard to estimate precisely" rather than inflating the number).
%
What it is: the % of customers you had 12 months ago who are still paying customers today. How to calculate: (customers still active today who were active 12 months ago) ÷ (total customers active 12 months ago) × 100.
1-5
What it is: how painful it would be for a customer to leave you for a competitor, on a 1-5 scale (1 = trivial to switch, 5 = deeply integrated, high switching pain). How to calculate: score based on actual customer behavior and integration depth, not aspiration.
