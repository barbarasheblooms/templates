MoM Growth & Retention Calculator
The two numbers that tell an investor whether your growth is real and durable
Before you start: by Seed, investors want to see growth that's both real (month-over-month MRR growth, annualized) and durable (customers who stay rather than churn out the back door). A high growth rate next to high churn tells a different story than the same growth rate with low churn. This tool calculates both from your real numbers.

Retention
What it is: the percentage of customers who cancel in an average month. How to calculate: customers lost in the month ÷ customers you had at the start of that month × 100. Use your last 1-3 months' average, not one unusual month.
