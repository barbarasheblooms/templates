Channel CAC Comparison Calculator
Find out which channel is actually working, with real numbers
Before you start: at Pre-Seed, "we tried a few channels" isn't a data point investors can use. What they want is a real cost-per-customer for each channel you tested, side by side. This tool takes what you spent and what you got from each of up to three channels and tells you which one is actually cheap and which one is quietly burning cash.
