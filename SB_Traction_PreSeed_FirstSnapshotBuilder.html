Activation & Stickiness Snapshot
Turn your first usage numbers into the two ratios investors check
Before you start: at Pre-Seed you rarely have months of clean cohort data, and that's fine. What you do have is signups, a key first action, and some sense of who comes back. This tool turns those into your activation rate and your stickiness ratio (WAU/MAU), the two earliest signals that people don't just try your product, they use it.
1
Signups and activation
What it is: everyone who has ever created an account. How to calculate: count total accounts created to date, including inactive ones. Use your product analytics or database, not a rounded guess.
What it is: users who completed the one action that proves they got real value (e.g. sent their first message, created their first project, made their first purchase). How to calculate: pick that single defining action, then count unique users who completed it at least once, out of total signups.
2
Weekly and monthly active users
What it is: unique users who did something meaningful in the product in the last 7 days. How to calculate: count distinct users with at least one qualifying action in a trailing 7-day window, from your analytics tool.
What it is: unique users who did something meaningful in the product in the last 30 days. How to calculate: count distinct users with at least one qualifying action in a trailing 30-day window, using the same qualifying action as your WAU number.
