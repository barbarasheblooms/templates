Expanded TAM Calculator
Show exactly how new segments or geographies grow your addressable market
Before you start: at Series A, "we're also expanding into X" is not an argument, it's a claim. Investors want to see the arithmetic behind it: how big the new segment is, and how much of it you could realistically capture given you haven't sold there yet. This tool adds up to two new segments or geographies onto your current TAM, using your own size and penetration estimates, so the expansion story on your slide is a calculation, not a gesture at the map.

Your current market
What it is: the size of the market you're already counting today, before adding any new segments or geographies. How to calculate: use the TAM figure from your most recent sizing work, don't re-derive it here, this tool builds on top of it.

New segment or geography 1
What it is: what you're calling this new segment or geography. How to calculate: name it specifically enough that an investor could picture the buyer, e.g. "UK mid-market retailers," not just "international."

What it is: the total addressable spend in this new segment, on its own, before factoring in how much of it you could actually capture. How to calculate: use the same bottom-up method as your original TAM: number of potential customers in this segment × what they'd pay per year.
%
What it is: the realistic % of that segment's total spend you could capture, given you haven't sold into it yet. How to calculate: look at your penetration rate in your proven, existing segment as a ceiling, then discount it, an unproven segment is harder to capture at the same rate as customers who already trust you.
