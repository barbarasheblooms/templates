Burn Multiple & Sensitivity Worksheet
The efficiency metric growth investors check before they check your growth rate
Before you start: Bessemer Venture Partners popularized the "burn multiple": how much cash you burn for every dollar of new recurring revenue you add. A fast-growing company that burns €3 for every €1 of new ARR is not efficient, no matter how good the growth headline looks. This tool calculates your burn multiple against Bessemer's own benchmark, and stress-tests your ARR against a worse churn scenario so you know how exposed the number is.

What it is: your total annualized recurring revenue right now. How to calculate: current MRR × 12, or sum of active annual contracts.

What it is: the annual churn rate you expect under normal conditions. How to calculate: ARR lost to cancellations and downgrades over the last 12 months ÷ ARR at the start of that period × 100.

What it is: a worse, still-plausible churn rate you'd want to survive. How to calculate: pick a rate meaningfully above your base case, informed by your worst historical quarter or a comparable company's downside scenario, not an arbitrary round number.
