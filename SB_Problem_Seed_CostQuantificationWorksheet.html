Cost of the Problem Calculator
Turn your customers' baseline pain into a euro number, the way Seed investors expect
Before you start: "this problem is really painful" is not evidence, a euro figure is. At Seed, investors expect you to have quantified what the problem actually costs your customers before your product existed, and how much of that cost you demonstrably remove. This tool turns your customers' baseline time and money into an aggregate monthly cost, and separates that from the (smaller, more credible) amount you've actually proven you recover.
1
The baseline, before your product
hours
What it is: how long the task or pain point took before your product, each time it happened. How to calculate: use customer-reported baselines from onboarding calls or discovery interviews, or clock an average from your own usage data from before customers switched to your product.
€/hr
What it is: what that time is worth to your customer, their billing rate or a reasonable estimate of the value of their time. How to calculate: use their actual hourly billing rate if they bill by the hour, or their fully-loaded hourly cost if they're an employee (annual salary ÷ roughly 2,000 working hours).
/mo
What it is: how many times per month this problem happens, per customer. How to calculate: ask customers directly how often they hit this, or count instances in your own usage logs over a typical month.
customers
What it is: how many of your paying customers actually deal with this specific problem. How to calculate: count active paying accounts where this problem applies, don't use your full customer base if only a subset experiences it.
2
What your product removes
%
What it is: the share of the baseline time or cost your product actually eliminates, measured from usage data. How to calculate: (baseline time − time spent using your product) ÷ baseline time × 100, measured across a sample of real customers, not assumed. Leave blank or enter 0 if you haven't measured this yet.
