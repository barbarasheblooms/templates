Infrastructure Efficiency at Scale
Check whether your costs improve or worsen as you grow, before an investor asks
Before you start: at Series A, investors aren't just checking that you have users, they're checking whether serving twice as many users costs you proportionally less, the same, or more. This tool compares your cost to serve one user today against your engineering team's honest projection at 2x scale, and tells you which of those three you're actually in.

What it is: your total infrastructure and support cost divided by your current user count, per month. How to calculate: total monthly infra + hosting + third-party API + support costs ÷ current active users. Use your last full month's actuals, not an estimate.
users
What it is: your current active user count, the denominator you used above. How to calculate: count active users (or accounts) in the same period you used to calculate cost to serve above, so the two numbers line up.

At 2x scale
€/mo
What it is: your engineering team's honest estimate of cost per user once you're at roughly double today's user count, accounting for infra changes, caching, batching, and any volume pricing you'd actually renegotiate. How to calculate: ask engineering to re-run today's cost model at 2x load with the specific technical changes you'd realistically make, not the same architecture scaled up linearly.
users
What it is: the user count this projection is based on. How to calculate: this should be roughly double your users-today figure above; enter your actual planning number even if it's not exactly 2x.
