Sean Ellis Test — PMF Measurement
The Sean Ellis Test is the most widely used PMF measurement framework. One question, sent to 40+ active users. If 40% or more answer "very disappointed", you have crossed from useful to essential. Below 40% — keep iterating.

"How would you feel if you could no longer use [product name]?"
